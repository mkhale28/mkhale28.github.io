02 — FLAGSHIP PROJECT

CairoCare Vault — blockchain-secured medical records

● GRADUATION PROJECT — TEAM OF 8

Patient records, encrypted, verified, and owned by the patient.

Built with a team of eight at the Faculty of Computers & AI, Benha University, under Dr. Ahmed Tawfik. CairoCare Vault stores medical records off-chain in encrypted form while using the blockchain to enforce access control and prove integrity — nobody, including the hospital, can quietly alter a record.

ReactDjango RESTEthereum / SolidityIPFSAES-256
  • Records encrypted with AES-256 and stored off-chain via IPFS
  • Smart contracts enforce who can view or grant access to a record
  • On-chain hashes make silent edits or tampering detectable
  • Isolation Forest model flags anomalous access patterns
  • React front end + Django REST API backend
